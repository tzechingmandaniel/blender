Section 3 Major Parts — Proportion + Silhouette Notes
======================================================

Section 3 turns the BLK_ blockout primitives from Section 2 into refined AK_
major parts. The goal is silhouette accuracy and clean topology, not surface
detail. Detail work (rivets, panel lines, sling loops, screw heads, magazine
ribs as real grooves, etc.) is deferred to Section 4.

Still strictly exterior-only — no internal mechanism, no functional barrel,
chamber, bolt, spring, trigger mechanism, firing system, detachable magazine
locking, or real assembly. All AK_ objects are decorative exterior meshes.

§3.1 Scope of "major part"
--------------------------
Per §2.4.2, the major parts that define overall proportion are:
  Receiver, Stock, Magazine, Grip, Lower handguard, Barrel exterior

Section 3 also refines the Upper handguard and Dust cover because they
dominate the upper silhouette. The sight blocks, gas block, front ring, and
trigger guard stay as BLK_ blockouts and are refined in Section 4.

§3.3 Per-part refinement targets
--------------------------------
3.3.1 Receiver
  - Length matches §2.7.2 receiver zone (X = -0.18 .. +0.05).
  - Top-rear corner slightly rounded toward the dust cover joint.
  - Bottom face has a shallow magazine-well silhouette (visual indentation
    only, no opening, no functional cut).
  - Side panel has a shallow ejection-port indentation (visual, surface
    deformation only).
  - Selector lever silhouette as a thin raised pad on the right side
    (right = +Y in this scene).
  - Trigger pocket on the underside is a shallow exterior recess.

3.3.2 Stock
  - Length matches §2.7.2 rear stock zone (X = -0.50 .. -0.18).
  - Buttplate (rear face) curved inward slightly (concave) along Z so the
    silhouette is the classic curved buttstock.
  - Cheek rest taper: top of stock slopes down from rear to where it meets
    the receiver.
  - Wrist (front, near receiver) narrows in both Y and Z.
  - One shallow sling slot silhouette on the lower-rear edge — visual
    indentation only, NOT a real hole.

3.3.3 Magazine
  - Banana curve more pronounced than the BLK_ version, smooth across 4+
    horizontal subdivisions.
  - Two decorative side ribs as raised bands on the +Y / -Y faces (no real
    grooves — purely raised silhouette).
  - Floorplate at the bottom slightly larger than the body footprint.
  - Top opening NOT modelled as an opening — it stays closed (this is a
    non-functional prop).

3.3.4 Grip
  - Pistol-grip contour: front face slightly concave (finger relief),
    rear face slightly convex (palm swell).
  - Top narrower than the bottom (already true in BLK_), with the taper
    smoother across 2 subdivisions.
  - Tilted ~18° back, same as the BLK_ tilt.
  - Flat cap at the bottom (no working screw, no working takedown).

3.3.5 Lower handguard
  - Classic AK lower handguard silhouette — front wider/taller than rear.
  - Two shallow finger grooves on the lower face (raised ridges either side
    of each groove, visual only).
  - Front cap is a thin raised collar joining the lower handguard to the
    barrel area.

3.3.6 Upper handguard
  - Slightly rounded top profile (already partially true in BLK_).
  - Rear end terminates in a small thumb-shelf raise.
  - No vent slots, no working heat shield (exterior visual only).

3.3.7 Barrel exterior
  - Straight cylinder along X, slightly tapered toward the muzzle.
  - Decorative collar near the muzzle end (NOT a working muzzle device,
    NOT a thread — just a visual collar).
  - No chamber, no bore hole, no real opening: muzzle face is a closed
    decorative disc.

3.3.8 Dust cover
  - Rounded top profile across the receiver length.
  - Rear edge dips slightly to meet the stock wrist (visual continuity).
  - Front edge has a small raised lip toward the rear sight area.
  - No real opening, no working catch, no internal parts visible.

§3.4 Topology + modifier rules
------------------------------
  - All AK_ objects have a Bevel modifier (Angle limit ~30°, width 0.002,
    2 segments).
  - All AK_ objects have a Weighted Normal modifier (weight 50, threshold
    0.01, keep sharp).
  - All AK_ objects start as a primitive then receive 1–3 loop cuts /
    subdivisions before the silhouette deformations.
  - No object-level booleans; all silhouette features are vertex moves or
    additive geometry.
  - Each AK_ object has its origin at its geometric center (apply
    transform after build, then re-parent).

§3.5 Collection + assembly rules
--------------------------------
  - All AK_ objects live in 03_MAJOR_PARTS.
  - All AK_ objects are parented to AK47_MASTER_ASSET (the empty created in
    the §2.5.1 step 14 supplement).
  - Section 3 hides 02_BLOCKOUT in the viewport (but keeps it in the file
    for reference); render visibility off as well.
  - Section 3 does NOT touch the BLK_ minor parts (sights, gas block, front
    ring, trigger guard) — those carry forward into Section 4.

§3.6 Side-view proportion checks (run after every part)
-------------------------------------------------------
Same set as §2.7.3, plus:
  - Does the AK_ part hide the BLK_ blockout completely when both are on?
  - Does the AK_ part still respect the §2.7.2 proportion zone for X?
  - Are origins at geometric centers (for clean later transforms)?

§3.7 Out of scope for Section 3
-------------------------------
  - Rivets, screws, real openings (all Section 4).
  - Sling loop, magazine catch, dust cover catch (all Section 4 visuals).
  - Surface panel lines, magazine real grooves (all Section 4).
  - Realistic materials (Section 5).
  - Final lighting and renders (Section 6).
