Section 7 Final Scene / Cameras / Lights / Renders / Turntable / Export
=======================================================================

Section 7 takes the Phase 6 textured-and-lookdev'd model and produces the
final presentation outputs: portfolio still renders, a 360° turntable
animation, and clean export files. No new geometry — only scene-level
additions (cameras, lights, background, turntable empty, export
collection) and render output.

Strictly exterior, strictly non-functional. No bore, chamber, working
trigger, functional magazine, real openings, manufacturing detail.

§7.1 Source / output
--------------------
Input:  01_blender/v06_final_textures_lookdev.blend
Output: 01_blender/v07_final_scene_export.blend

§7.3 Master assembly
--------------------
AK47_MASTER_ASSET already exists (from Section 3) and parents every AK_
object. AK47_MASTER_ASSEMBLY already exists (from Section 5) inside
07_ASSEMBLY. For the turntable rotation, we add:
  EMPTY_turntable_center  — at world origin (model already centered).
  EMPTY_turntable_center is parented to nothing.
  AK47_MASTER_ASSET (which already parents all AK_ parts) is parented to
  EMPTY_turntable_center so rotating the empty rotates the whole prop
  without modifying any AK_ object transforms.

§7.8 Turntable animation
------------------------
File: 04_renders/final/turntable_ak_style_prop.mp4
180 frames at 24 fps (7.5 s loop), linear interpolation, full 360° rotation
of EMPTY_turntable_center around Z. Camera static. Background visible.
For headless execution, render the animation as PNG frames into
04_renders/final/turntable_frames/ and either encode separately or accept
the frame sequence as the deliverable. A 12-frame low-resolution preview
(960×540, every 15° step) is rendered first for sanity, saved as
turntable_preview_12frames.mp4 (or PNG sequence).

§7.9 Export-ready collection (09_EXPORT_READY)
----------------------------------------------
Mirror only the AK_ exterior prop objects into 09_EXPORT_READY/
EXPORT_AK47_EXTERIOR_PROP_MASTER. No cameras, no lights, no references,
no blockout, no material-test geometry. Materials and UV layers are
preserved.

§7.10 Export files (02_exports/)
--------------------------------
  AK47_exterior_prop_visual.fbx
  AK47_exterior_prop_visual.glb
  AK47_exterior_prop_visual.obj

Each is exported with selection-only from the export-ready collection,
with applied modifiers, embedded materials, and preserved UV layer. No
mesh decimation. No baked textures (procedural shaders are preserved in
the .blend; exporters will use either Principled BSDF defaults or write a
flat color depending on format).
