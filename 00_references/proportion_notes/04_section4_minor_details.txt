Section 4 Minor Exterior Details — Plan + Density Budget
=========================================================

Section 4 adds decorative exterior surface details to the AK_ major parts
from Section 3. Strictly exterior, strictly decorative, strictly non-
functional. No internal mechanism, no working trigger, no chamber, bolt,
spring, firing system, functional magazine system, bore, rifling, threaded
muzzle, real screw holes, real openings, manufacturing dimensions, or
real assembly. Rivets, panel lines, ribs, grooves, contours, sling loops,
and edge-wear placeholders are all surface visuals only.

§4.5 Per-detail geometry rules
------------------------------
Rivet:           cylinder, radius 0.0015 m, depth 0.003 m, sitting on +Y or
                 +Z surface so the dome pops out ~0.002 m.
Panel line:      thin elongated box ~(0.040 × 0.0010 × 0.012) raised
                 against +Y face of the receiver.
Seam marker:     very thin elongated box (~0.0008 m on its short axis),
                 hugging a boundary between two parts.
Magazine groove: a thin horizontal band wrapping across the +X (front)
                 face of the magazine (dims ~(0.0015 × 0.030 × 0.005)).
Magazine spine: a thin vertical ridge running along the front or rear
                 face (dims ~(0.003 × 0.012 × 0.090)).
Wood contour:    raised band along the length of the wood part
                 (~(L × 0.0008 × 0.002) lifted against +Z or -Z surface).
Edge wear:       a thin scuff strip following an exposed edge (~(L × 0.002
                 × 0.0006)).
Grip groove:     a thin horizontal band on the front face of the grip
                 (~(0.0015 × 0.020 × 0.004)).
Sling loop:      torus, major radius ~0.008, minor radius ~0.0015.
Front sight cap: a small box ~(0.008 × 0.004 × 0.003) on top of the front
                 sight block.

§4.6 Placement principles
-------------------------
1. Every detail is placed in WORLD coordinates relative to a known surface
   on its host AK_ part. Origin is the geometric centre of the detail.
2. Details sit ON the surface — never floating, never deeply embedded.
   Acceptable embed depth ≤ 0.0005 m so the bevel modifier on the host
   does not visually fight them.
3. Repeated details (rivets, grooves) are evenly spaced along a single
   axis. No random scatter.
4. No detail is wider than its host face. No detail makes a visible
   "cut" or "hole".
5. All detail objects are parented to AK47_MASTER_ASSET so the whole
   model transforms as one unit.
6. All detail objects have a Bevel modifier (small width 0.0005 m, 1 seg,
   angle 30°) — keep the bevel cheap because these are tiny.

§4.7 Out of scope for Section 4
-------------------------------
- Final textures, UV layouts, wood grain (Section 5).
- Final scratches, dirt maps, baked AO (Section 5–6).
- Real openings, working catches, working sights (never — exterior prop).
- Real screws (only seam markers and rivets, both decorative).
- Internal exposure of any kind.
