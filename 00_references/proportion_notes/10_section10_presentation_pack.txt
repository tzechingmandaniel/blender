Section 10 Advanced Portfolio Presentation Pack
================================================

Section 10 produces the advanced_presentation_pack/ folder from the
existing final_delivery/ + post_delivery/. This is a public-facing
presentation finishing phase. No Blender, no renders, no model edits.

Strictly visual non-functional exterior prop. No internal mechanism,
no functional anything, no manufacturing dimensions.

§10.3 Reusable presentation template strategy
---------------------------------------------
Generic [ProjectName] placeholder replaces "AK47_NonFunctional_Prop".
Object-specific wording ("dark blued metal", "bakelite grip") becomes
"<material 1>", "<material 2>", etc. The template is portable to any
future non-functional exterior prop project.
