UV seam strategy (per §5.6):
  Receiver / dust cover  -> seam loop on the bottom perimeter (least visible).
  Stock / lower hg       -> seam along -Z bottom edge, +X = +U.
  Upper hg               -> seam along +Z top edge (faces down when mounted).
  Magazine               -> seam on rear spine + horizontal seam at baseplate.
  Grip                   -> seam on the +X front face (front grip surface).
  Barrel                 -> cylinder seam on -Z bottom strip; +X = +U.
  Small details          -> Smart UV Project at angle 66°, margin 0.02.

Wood grain direction (per §5.7):

  grain runs lengthwise from butt to receiver / front to rear.
