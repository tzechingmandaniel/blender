Phase 6 Prompt 10 — Subtle Dirt + Weathering
============================================

Drive a "grime" tint by inverting the CavityAO output (cavities = more
grime). Multiply the Base Color of the 4 hero materials toward a dark
neutral (0.18, 0.16, 0.14) using `grime_factor` per material:

  MAT_metal_dark_blued     0.30
  MAT_metal_black_magazine 0.35
  MAT_wood_dark_reddish    0.25
  MAT_grip_dark_bakelite   0.30

Effect:
  - Cavities and grooves pick up subtle grime
  - Flat faces stay clean (Pointiness near 0.5 returns no grime)
  - No combat blood, no aggressive context

Hard scope: no violent context, no exposed internal dirt, no functional
wear marks. Keep dirt restrained — the prop should still read clearly.

After running, verify:
  [ ] Each hero material multiplies Base Color toward the grime tint
  [ ] Grime factor is restrained (<= 0.35)
  [ ] Materials remain readable, not muddy
