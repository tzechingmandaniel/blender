Phase 6 Prompt 9 — Subtle Scratches + Roughness Variation
=========================================================

Inside each of the 4 hero materials, blend a fine Noise texture into the
Roughness input via a Mix node:

  Roughness_base + Noise_fine * variation_strength

variation_strength per material:
  MAT_metal_dark_blued     0.18  (visible roughness variation)
  MAT_metal_black_magazine 0.12  (slightly more uniform)
  MAT_wood_dark_reddish    0.10  (mild varnish variation)
  MAT_grip_dark_bakelite   0.06  (very subtle)

Also drive the Bump node with the same Noise (strength as in §6.3) so the
roughness variation reads as fine surface relief, not pure shader noise.

Keep scratches subtle and material-based — do not carve geometry, do not
generate deep dark scratches, do not cover the whole surface with bright
streaks.

Hard scope: no geometric scratches, no real damage.

After running, verify:
  [ ] Each hero material has a Noise -> Roughness mix
  [ ] Each hero material's Bump uses the same Noise source
  [ ] No new geometry edits
