Phase 5 Prompt 8 — Texel Density Check
======================================

Target texel density: 1024 px per metre.

For every major AK_ object, compute the ratio of (UV area in 0..1²) /
(world surface area). The expected ratio at the target density and 1024 px
maps is ~1.0 — but values 0.5..2.0 are acceptable for now.

Visible large surfaces (must hit 0.7×–1.3× target):
  AK_receiver_main, AK_stock_main, AK_handguard_lower_main,
  AK_handguard_upper_main, AK_magazine_main, AK_grip_main,
  AK_barrel_exterior_main, AK_dustcover_main

Small details may use any density.

Do not edit UVs in this prompt — only measure and report. Write the
findings into 06_documentation/uv_notes.txt under the heading "Texel
density check".

Hard scope: report-only. Do not modify geometry or UVs.

After running, verify:
  [ ] Report saved into uv_notes.txt
  [ ] All 8 major parts listed with measured ratios
  [ ] Any object outside 0.5..2.0 is flagged
