MCP Prompt §4.12 — Magazine Exterior Details
=============================================

Work around AK_magazine_main and create separate decorative detail objects
inside 04_MINOR_PARTS / 04C_magazine_details. Strictly exterior, decorative,
non-functional. Do not create feed lips, internal cartridge space, spring,
follower, locking tabs, ammunition, functional magazine interface, magazine
release mechanism, manufacturing details, or technical assembly.

Detail set:
- 4 broad horizontal grooves wrapping across the +X (front) face of the
  magazine, following the banana curve:
    AK_groove_magazine_001 at world Z ≈ -0.045.
    AK_groove_magazine_002 at world Z ≈ -0.085.
    AK_groove_magazine_003 at world Z ≈ -0.125.
    AK_groove_magazine_004 at world Z ≈ -0.165.
  Each groove is a thin raised band ~(0.0015 × 0.030 × 0.005). The X-world
  position follows the banana curl (front face moves +X by ~+0.030 to
  ~+0.080 as Z drops). Approximate placements:
    001: world (-0.005, 0, -0.045)
    002: world (+0.005, 0, -0.085)
    003: world (+0.030, 0, -0.125)
    004: world (+0.045, 0, -0.165)
- 1 vertical front spine ridge along the +X face:
    AK_spine_magazine_front_001, dims ~(0.003 × 0.012 × 0.090), centred
    around world (+0.015, 0, -0.110).
- 1 vertical rear spine ridge along the -X face:
    AK_spine_magazine_rear_001, dims ~(0.003 × 0.012 × 0.090), centred
    around world (-0.100, 0, -0.105).
- 1 baseplate seam marker, a thin band at the join between magazine body
  and the floorplate:
    AK_seam_magazine_baseplate_001, dims ~(0.118 × 0.062 × 0.0008),
    centred around world (+0.010, 0, -0.175).

Naming:
AK_groove_magazine_001 .. 004
AK_spine_magazine_front_001
AK_spine_magazine_rear_001
AK_seam_magazine_baseplate_001

Materials:
- Grooves, spines:  MAT_placeholder_black_metal
- Baseplate seam:   MAT_placeholder_shadow_seam

All objects parented to AK47_MASTER_ASSET. Bevel modifier (width 0.0005,
segments 1, angle 30°) on every object.

After running:
[ ] Magazine details are in 04C_magazine_details.
[ ] Detail count = 7 (4 grooves + 2 spines + 1 baseplate seam).
[ ] Grooves follow the banana curve roughly.
[ ] No feed lip, no internal cartridge space, no functional element.
