MCP Prompt §4.8 — Receiver Exterior Details
=============================================

Work around AK_receiver_main and create separate decorative detail objects
inside 04_MINOR_PARTS / 04A_receiver_details. Strictly exterior, decorative,
non-functional. Do not create internal openings, bolt paths, chamber details,
trigger mechanisms, functional selector mechanisms, real ejection port
cutouts, internal rails, manufacturing dimensions, or technical assembly.

Detail set (density budget caps from §4.4):
- 6 decorative rivet domes on the +Y face of the receiver. Cylinders
  ~0.0015 m radius × 0.003 m depth, dome facing +Y. Place along two
  rows at z = +0.018 and z = -0.018, at receiver world-X positions
  -0.155, -0.075, +0.005 (so 3 rivets per row × 2 rows = 6 rivets).
- 2 broad shallow side panel lines on the +Y face:
    AK_panel_receiver_side_001 around the ejection-port silhouette area.
    AK_panel_receiver_side_002 ahead of the magazine well.
  Each is a thin elongated box ~(0.040 × 0.0010 × 0.012) sitting on the
  +Y face (y ≈ +0.021).
- 1 receiver/dust-cover top seam marker (AK_seam_receiver_top_001), a
  very thin box ~(0.220 × 0.034 × 0.0008) hugging the line where the
  dust cover meets the receiver top (world z ≈ +0.039).
- 2 boundary seam markers:
    AK_seam_receiver_stock_001 (vertical line at receiver/stock joint,
                                world X ≈ -0.180).
    AK_seam_receiver_handguard_001 (vertical line at receiver/handguard
                                joint, world X ≈ +0.050).

Naming:
AK_rivet_receiver_001 .. 006
AK_panel_receiver_side_001 .. 002
AK_seam_receiver_top_001
AK_seam_receiver_stock_001
AK_seam_receiver_handguard_001

Materials:
- Rivets, panel lines:  MAT_placeholder_dark_metal
- Seam markers:         MAT_placeholder_shadow_seam

All objects parented to AK47_MASTER_ASSET. Bevel modifier (width 0.0005,
segments 1, angle 30°) on every object.

After running:
[ ] All receiver details are in 04A_receiver_details.
[ ] Rivet count = 6, panel count = 2, seam count = 3.
[ ] No detail floats — every detail sits on the receiver surface.
[ ] No functional opening or internal exposure exists.
