MCP Mega Prompt §4.31 — Full Section 4 (Ready-to-Use)
=======================================================

Open the existing v03_major_parts.blend (Section 3 result) and immediately
save the file as v04_minor_details.blend at the same path.

Important safety and scope:
- This is only a visual exterior prop.
- Do not create internal firearm mechanisms.
- Do not create functional barrel, chamber, bolt, spring, firing system,
  detachable magazine system, working trigger, bore, rifling, threaded
  muzzle, real screw holes, real openings, or manufacturing details.
- All Section 4 objects must be solid decorative exterior placeholders
  only.

Task:
Add ~50 minor exterior detail objects across 8 functional sub-collections
under 04_MINOR_PARTS. Detail counts (hard caps) and placements come from
§4.4–§4.22. No object beyond the cap is added in the primary pass; if
extras appear, they go into 04Z_detail_tests_archive.

Run order:
 1. Setup    — create the 9 sub-collections inside 04_MINOR_PARTS and the
              two new placeholder materials MAT_placeholder_shadow_seam
              and MAT_placeholder_edge_wear (§4.4).
 2. Receiver — 6 rivets, 2 panel lines, 3 seam markers in 04A
              (§4.8).
 3. Dust cover — 3 ribs, 2 side seams in 04B (§4.10).
 4. Magazine — 4 grooves, 2 spines, 1 baseplate seam in 04C (§4.12).
 5. Wood    — 2 stock + 2 lower-hg + 2 upper-hg contour bands, 3 wood
              edge-wear placeholders in 04D (§4.14).
 6. Grip    — 3 grooves, 1 base-cap seam in 04E, all rotated -18° to
              follow the tilted grip front (§4.16).
 7. Sights  — 1 front-sight cap, 1 gas-block seam, 1 front ring in 04F
              (§4.18).
 8. Sling loops — 2 torus visuals in 04G (§4.20).
 9. Seam + edge-wear placeholders — 5 seam markers, 3 metal-edge wear,
              2 wood-edge wear in 04H (§4.22).
10. Density cleanup — confirm caps, parenting, materials, bevels;
              archive any surplus into 04Z (§4.24).

Naming rules:
- All detail objects start with AK_.
- Each contains one of the detail keywords: rivet, panel, rib, seam,
  groove, spine, contour, loop, wear, cap, ring.
- No default Cube.001 / Cylinder.002 names.

Material rules:
- Metal details:     MAT_placeholder_dark_metal
- Magazine details:  MAT_placeholder_black_metal
- Wood details:      MAT_placeholder_dark_wood
- Grip grooves:      MAT_placeholder_bakelite_grip
- Seam markers:      MAT_placeholder_shadow_seam (NEW in Section 4)
- Edge-wear placeholders: MAT_placeholder_edge_wear (NEW in Section 4)

Assembly rules:
- All detail objects parented to AK47_MASTER_ASSET.
- All detail objects have a Bevel modifier (width 0.0005, segments 1,
  angle 30°).
- The 04Z_detail_tests_archive sub-collection is hidden in viewport
  and render.

Section 4 finishes when the §4.29 final review checklist passes (see
section4_verify.py). Save as v04_minor_details.blend at the same path
as the v03 file.
