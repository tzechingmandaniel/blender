Section 3 — Grip and Trigger Guard Prompt (§3.9, §3.16, §3.19.4)
=================================================================

Refine the pistol grip and trigger guard visual parts. AK_grip_main and
AK_trigger_guard_visual already exist as duplicates in 03_MAJOR_PARTS/03C_grip.

Grip (AK_grip_main):
  1. Keep the existing backward tilt from BLK_grip_main (~-18°).
  2. Round front and back vertical edges with a larger bevel.
  3. Modifier stack:
       Bevel    : width=0.0030, segments=3, limit=ANGLE 35°  (rounded
                  ergonomic feel per §3.18.2)
       Weighted normal : weight=50, threshold=0.01, keep_sharp=True
  4. Material: MAT_placeholder_bakelite_grip (single slot).
  5. Add optional AK_grip_basecap_visual as a separate object:
       - thin box, dims ≈ (0.050, 0.034, 0.005)
       - located at the bottom face of the grip
       - material: MAT_placeholder_black_metal
       - linked to 03C_grip
       - parented to AK47_MASTER_ASSET
       - keep it thin and decorative (no functional screw or internal detail).

Trigger guard (AK_trigger_guard_visual):
  1. Keep the simple curved silhouette from BLK_trigger_guard_visual.
  2. Modifier stack:
       Bevel    : width=0.0015, segments=2, limit=ANGLE 30°
       Weighted normal : weight=50, threshold=0.01, keep_sharp=True
  3. Material: MAT_placeholder_dark_metal (single slot).
  4. Confirm visual position: below receiver, in front of grip, no overlap
     with magazine.

Do not add:
  - Checkered texture or fine grip grooves (deferred).
  - Real screw detail or internal grip structure.
  - Working trigger mechanism, internal linkage, or pivot points.

Stop at these parts.
