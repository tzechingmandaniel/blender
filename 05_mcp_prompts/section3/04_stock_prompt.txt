Section 3 — Stock Prompt (§3.8, §3.19.3)
=========================================

Refine only the wooden buttstock exterior major part. AK_stock_main already
exists as a duplicate in 03_MAJOR_PARTS/03B_stock.

Tasks for AK_stock_main:
  1. Confirm the existing taper from BLK_stock_main is preserved
     (front toward receiver narrower than rear butt end).
  2. Round the rear butt end (apply a slightly larger bevel to the rear
     vertical edges).
  3. Apply modifier stack:
       Bevel    : width=0.0035, segments=3, limit=ANGLE 35°  (larger rounded
                  bevel, wood feel per §3.18.2)
       Weighted normal : weight=50, threshold=0.01, keep_sharp=True
  4. Material: MAT_placeholder_dark_wood (single slot).
  5. Add optional decorative AK_stock_buttplate_visual as a separate object:
       - thin box, dims ≈ (0.012, 0.040, 0.085)
       - located at the rear of the stock, oriented along the butt face
       - material: MAT_placeholder_black_metal
       - linked to 03B_stock
       - parented to AK47_MASTER_ASSET
       - keep it solid and decorative (no functional attachment).

Do not add:
  - Wood grain or final texture (deferred to Section 5).
  - Screw detail or functional mounting structure.
  - Real internal attachment geometry.

Sanity-check side and 3/4 view: stock has thickness, top curve is natural,
lower angled edge matches reference.

Stop at this part.
