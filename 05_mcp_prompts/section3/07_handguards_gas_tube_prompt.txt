Section 3 — Handguards and Gas Tube Prompt (§3.11, §3.12, §3.19.6)
===================================================================

Refine only the lower handguard, upper handguard, and gas tube exterior
parts. AK_handguard_lower_main, AK_handguard_upper_main, and
AK_gas_tube_outer already exist as duplicates in 03_MAJOR_PARTS/03E_handguard.

Lower handguard (AK_handguard_lower_main):
  - Modifier stack:
      Bevel    : width=0.0030, segments=3, limit=ANGLE 35°  (rounded wood feel)
      Weighted normal : weight=50, threshold=0.01, keep_sharp=True
  - Material: MAT_placeholder_dark_wood (single slot).
  - Confirm slight lower contour from BLK_ source is preserved.

Upper handguard (AK_handguard_upper_main):
  - Modifier stack: same as lower handguard.
  - Material: MAT_placeholder_dark_wood (single slot).
  - Confirm visual sits above lower handguard with no merge.

Gas tube exterior (AK_gas_tube_outer):
  - Modifier stack:
      Bevel    : width=0.0010, segments=2, limit=ANGLE 30°  (small bevel on
                 cylinder ends per §3.18.2)
      Weighted normal : weight=50, threshold=0.01, keep_sharp=True
  - Material: MAT_placeholder_dark_metal (single slot).
  - Do not add gas port or any internal tube detail.

Optional decorative collars, each a separate object parented to
AK47_MASTER_ASSET, linked to 03E_handguard, material MAT_placeholder_dark_metal:

  AK_handguard_upper_front_collar_visual
    - thin ring/cylinder, radius ≈ 0.013, depth ≈ 0.012, axis X
    - position: at the front end of AK_handguard_upper_main, around the
      barrel/gas tube line.

  AK_handguard_upper_rear_collar_visual
    - thin ring/cylinder, same dims as the front collar
    - position: at the rear end of AK_handguard_upper_main.

Sanity-check: wood and metal parts read as separate; no internal gas-system
detail; nothing collides badly with the barrel exterior.

Stop at these parts.
