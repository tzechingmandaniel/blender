Scope and Safety Note
Non-functional AK-style Exterior 3D Prop
============================================================

This project is a non-functional exterior 3D visual prop, created in
Blender for portfolio presentation, hard-surface modelling practice,
material lookdev, and game-asset display.

It does NOT include:
- internal firearm mechanisms
- a working trigger system
- chamber, bolt, spring, or firing components
- a functional magazine system
- bore, rifling, or functional barrel details
- manufacturing dimensions or tolerances
- blueprint-level technical modelling
- real weapon assembly instructions
- functional disassembly details
- moving mechanical weapon parts

The model is intended only for visual rendering, portfolio presentation,
and game-asset display. Any resemblance to a real firearm is silhouette-
and surface-level only — there is no functional, mechanical, or
manufacturing content in the model or in its documentation.
