THUMBNAIL
---------
Source:      Crop from 02_front_3quarter_hero.png
Crop rules:
- Focus on full prop silhouette — keep the whole prop in frame.
- Show metal/wood material contrast clearly (both groups visible).
- Do not cut off too much of the model — silhouette must read.
- No text if the image is strong on its own.
- Optional small text: "Exterior 3D Prop".
- Readable at small size — check at 200x200 px and at 100x100 px before
  approving.


THUMBNAIL RULES
---------------
1. Use a neutral background.
2. Avoid aggressive scene context (tactical staging, action poses, etc.).
3. Avoid technical-diagram look (no callouts, no measurement lines).
4. Avoid showing the prop as usable.
5. Keep it clean and professional.
6. Do not include dimensions or blueprint overlays.
