IMAGE SEQUENCE RULES
--------------------
1. Start with strongest hero image.
2. Show full model before close-ups.
3. Show close-ups by material group (metal → wood → magazine → grip → wear).
4. Put optional technical/art breakdown (clay, wireframe) near the end.
5. End with turntable.
6. Do not include weak renders — if a render is weak, remove it from the
   sequence rather than burying it.
7. Do not include unsafe technical views (cutaways, internals, blueprints).
